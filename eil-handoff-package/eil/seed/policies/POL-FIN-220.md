### 1.1 Scope
This policy establishes monetary authorisation thresholds for all committed enterprise
expenditure, irrespective of the requesting function or the system through which the
expenditure is raised.

### 3.1 High-value threshold
Any single committed expenditure with a total value exceeding INR 25,000 requires
authorisation by the Finance Director in addition to any functional approval required
under the owning policy. Functional approval does not substitute for Finance authorisation.

### 3.2 Aggregation
Where a request comprises multiple line items forming a single commercial commitment,
the threshold in clause 3.1 applies to the aggregate value, not to individual lines.

### 3.4 Sequencing
Where both functional and Finance authorisation are required, functional approval is
obtained first. Finance authorisation is sought only on a functionally approved request.

### 4.1 Delegation
The Finance Director may delegate authorisation up to INR 100,000 to a nominated deputy.
Delegations must be recorded and are valid for a maximum of 90 days.

### 5.2 Recurring commitments
Subscriptions and recurring licences are assessed on annualised value for the purpose
of clause 3.1.
