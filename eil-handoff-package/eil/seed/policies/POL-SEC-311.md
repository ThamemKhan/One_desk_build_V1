### 2.1 Definition
Privileged access means any credential permitting schema modification, bulk export or
credential administration on a production system.

### 2.4 Grade eligibility
Privileged production access may be granted only to employees at grade G5 or above who
hold a current security attestation. Requests from employees below grade G5 must be
declined and referred to the reporting manager.

### 2.7 No automated grant
Privileged access is never granted by an automated process. A named human authority must
record the decision.

### 3.1 Recertification
Privileged access is recertified every 90 days. Access not recertified is revoked
automatically.
