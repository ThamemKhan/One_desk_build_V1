### 1.1 Purpose
This policy governs all business travel undertaken by employees, including transport,
lodging, per diem and incidental expenses. It applies to all grades and all locations.

### 2.1 Advance approval
All business travel must be approved before booking. Travel booked without prior approval
is reimbursed at the discretion of Travel Operations and may be declined.

### 3.1 City tiers
For the purpose of lodging entitlement, cities are classified as Tier 1 (Mumbai, Delhi,
Bengaluru, Hyderabad, Chennai, Kolkata, Pune) or Tier 2 (all other domestic locations).
Tier is determined by the destination city, not the employee's home location.

### 5.3 Lodging caps
Nightly lodging expenditure must not exceed INR 10,000 per night for Tier 1 destinations
and INR 8,000 per night for Tier 2 destinations. Caps are inclusive of taxes and
mandatory service charges.

### 5.4 Per diem
Meal and incidental per diem is INR 1,800 per day for Tier 1 destinations and
INR 1,200 per day for Tier 2 destinations.

### 5.7 Lodging exceptions
Where no compliant accommodation is available, an employee may request an exception to
clause 5.3. Exceptions to lodging caps are approved by Travel Operations. The requester
must attach evidence of unavailability. Approved exceptions apply only to the specific
trip and expire at the end of the travel period.

### 5.8 Exception evidence standard
Acceptable evidence of unavailability includes dated search results from an approved
booking channel showing no compliant inventory within 5 kilometres of the business location.

### 6.2 Advance booking window
Domestic travel should be booked at least seven calendar days in advance. Bookings made
inside this window may attract additional review.
