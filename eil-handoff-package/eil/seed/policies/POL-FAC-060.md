### 1.1 Scope
This policy covers workplace equipment, ergonomic assessments and facilities services.

### 2.3 Equipment entitlement
Employees may request standard ergonomic equipment once every 24 months without
additional justification.

### 2.6 Non-standard equipment
Non-standard equipment requires a documented ergonomic assessment and Facilities Manager
approval, and is subject to the Expenditure Authorisation Policy thresholds.
