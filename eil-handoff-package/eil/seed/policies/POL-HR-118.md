### 1.1 Scope
This policy covers annual leave, sick leave, onboarding and the employment-status
prerequisites for enterprise system access.

### 3.2 Annual leave approval
Annual leave is approved by the employee's reporting manager. Requests of three
consecutive working days or fewer, where sufficient balance exists, may be
auto-approved by the service platform with notification to the manager.

### 3.5 Leave balance
An employee may not book annual leave in excess of the accrued balance shown in the
human resources system. Negative balances require Head of Human Resources approval.

### 3.9 Notice period
Annual leave of more than five consecutive working days should be requested at least
fourteen calendar days in advance.

### 7.1 System access
All requests for enterprise system access are subject to review by Information Security.
Human Resources confirms employment status and reporting line as part of intake.
