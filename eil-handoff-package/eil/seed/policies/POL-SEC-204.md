### 1.1 Authority
This policy is issued by the Office of the Chief Information Security Officer. Where any
provision of this policy conflicts with an operational policy or standard operating
procedure, this policy prevails.

### 4.1 Access principle
Access to enterprise systems is granted on the principle of least privilege and only for
a documented business purpose.

### 4.2 Production data store access
Approval for access to any production data store rests with the Data Custodian for that
data domain. This authority may not be delegated to the application owner or to any
operational function. Version 3.1 of this policy withdraws the delegation previously
recorded at POL-DG-090 clause 2.2.

### 4.3 Human Resources involvement
Human Resources is responsible for verifying employment status only. Human Resources
approval is not required for the grant of technical system access.

### 4.6 Third-party tooling
Software that processes customer personal data requires a security review prior to
procurement or deployment, in addition to any approval required by the owning function.

### 5.1 Access duration
Standing production access is not granted. All production data access is time-bound and
must specify an expiry date not exceeding 90 days.

### 7.2 Emergency access
Emergency access may be granted by the Chief Information Security Officer for a maximum
of 24 hours and must be reviewed within two business days.
